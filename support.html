Two things that answer most questions

  • A session I finished doesn't appear in the record. Check that iCloud sync is on for the device that recorded it, and open the app once on that device.
  • Sync isn't moving between devices. Both devices must be signed into the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on. The app has no account of its own, so if iCloud is off there is nothing to sync through.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the App Store app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for. Refunds are issued by Apple, not by us: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove the device copy. To remove the iCloud copy: iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Focus Crab → Delete.
